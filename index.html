The Boutique

Fashion that already has a life to tell you about

Phindi’s Boutique started with pieces from my own wardrobe, clothes I no longer wore but still had life in them. It has grown into a curated space for second-hand pieces that deserve a second look. Every item is cleaned, styled, and photographed with care before being listed on Yaga for you to shop. Affordable, sustainable, and never boring.
