About Us

Hi, I'm Phindi

The face, hands, and closet behind the boutique.

Founder & Curator

Fashion shouldn't cost the earth — literally or figuratively

I started Phindi's Boutique on my bedroom floor, sorting through bags of second-hand clothes I'd collected from family, markets, and the occasional lucky find. What began as restyling old pieces for friends turned into something bigger: a small business built entirely on giving clothes a second life.

"Every item on this rail was loved once. I just want to help it get loved again."

Today, every piece I list is chosen by hand, steamed, styled, and photographed before it goes up for sale on Yaga. No mass production, no waste — just good clothes finding good homes.

— Phindi

What We Stand For

Style, Affordability, Sustainability

Three things guide every decision we make at the boutique — from what we buy to how we price it.

Style

Second-hand doesn't mean second best. Every piece is chosen for how it looks and feels, not just how cheap it was to source.

Affordability

Great fashion should be within reach. Our prices stay honest so you can build a wardrobe you love without the guilt.

Sustainability

Every purchase here is one less garment in landfill. Small choices, made often, add up to something that matters.
